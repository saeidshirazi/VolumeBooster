Tips & fullscreen

One click to boost. Choose a preset, use + / −, or drag the slider. Start gently and lower the level if audio crackles.

Fullscreen. Compatible players keep native fullscreen. Chromium browsers can limit fullscreen in tab audio mode; try F11. Firefox uses compatible players directly and has no tab audio fallback. Re-enable after a reload in native mode.

Keyboard. Alt + Shift + ↑ / ↓ changes the volume. Alt + Shift + M mutes. Enable this tab first. Change conflicting keys using Shortcuts.

Audio stays on your device. No recordings, uploads, or tracking. Protected media may not work. Your device and the site's own player must be unmuted.
